# NWO - Cybermobbing Cartel: Germany's Hidden Network

## 📖 Project Overview

This is a story project that exposes the operations of the NWO (New World Order) - Cybermobbing Cartel, a sophisticated global terrorist network that originated in Germany in 2020. The narrative reveals how an unlikely alliance between German rap musicians and VFX compositors—including one working at Industrial Light & Magic (Disney)—formed a new global terrorist organization. Early in their formation, the Russian GRU (Main Intelligence Directorate) identified their potential and established contact, providing substantial funding and intelligence support specifically to grow the group and attack German democracy from within. The group's sole objective is to form echo chambers of young people to radicalize them with Nazi ideology, using advanced digital visual effects capabilities, music industry influence, and Russian military intelligence backing to execute coordinated cybermobbing, psychological warfare, and systematic persecution across international borders.
