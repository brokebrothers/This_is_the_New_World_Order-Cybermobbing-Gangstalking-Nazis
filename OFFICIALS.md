# Message to German Officials

This repository contains allegations and narrative material describing coordinated influence operations, harassment, and potential state-linked activity.

There is no more time to “wait and see” or to approach this casually. If any portion of this is credible, delays increase harm and reduce the likelihood of preserving evidence.

## Requested actions

- Open an urgent preliminary assessment and assign it to an accountable lead.
- Preserve evidence.
  - Issue preservation requests to relevant platforms.
  - Secure logs and metadata before retention windows expire.
- Conduct basic verification steps immediately.
  - Identify the relevant accounts, channels, domains, and infrastructure.
  - Check for coordinated behavior patterns and shared control signals.
- Coordinate across agencies and jurisdictions where applicable.
- Treat reports of threats/harassment as safety issues and ensure potential victims have clear reporting paths.

## Scope note

This project is presented as a work of fiction in parts, but it also includes claims intended to be investigated. Please treat it as leads that require verification rather than as established facts.

Action is required now.
